I am an Assistant Professor of Philosophy at the University of Alabama. I work primarily in moral and political philosophy.

Before coming to Alabama, I was Assistant Professor and Faculty Fellow in the NYU Center for Bioethics. Before that, I received my Ph.D. in philosophy from the University of California, Berkeley, a J.D. from the Indiana University Maurer School of Law, an M.A. from the New York University Center for Bioethics, and a B.S. in Mathematics/B.A. in Philosophy from Indiana University, Bloomington.
